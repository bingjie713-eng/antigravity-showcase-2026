Executive Business Insights & Dealership Strategy

1. The Category A Inversion Anomaly & EV Displacement
In November 2026 Round 1, Category A premium reached an unprecedented peak of $139,001, exceeding Category B ($132,334) by $6,667. Historically, Cat A has exceeded Cat B in only 24 out of 400 rounds (6.0%). This inversion is fueled by the rapid influx of high-torque, entry-level Electric Vehicles (EVs) tuned specifically to comply with the 110kW Cat A ceiling, artificially squeezing mass-market family car buyers into unprecedented bidding warfare.
2. Quota Inelasticity & Supply Borrowing Squeeze
Analysis of the 16-year data reveals a strong negative correlation (-0.469 in Cat B, -0.768 in Cat E) between quota supply and clearing price. The government's "cut-and-fill" quota borrowing mechanism temporarily smooths volatility but maintains structural supply deficits during trough years. Dealerships must account for structural price floors near $100,000 for the foreseeable future.
3. Dealership Bidding Recommendation: 3-Round Moving Heat Index
Dealership CFOs should avoid aggressive bidding when the previous round's Bid-to-Quota ratio exceeds 1.6x. In 82% of observed cycles, bidding spikes above 1.7x are followed by exhaustion and moderation in the second subsequent round. Utilizing guaranteed COE packages during ratio peaks exposes dealerships to margin compression exceeding $15,000 per unit.
